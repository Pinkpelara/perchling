## About this software
Perchlings are small 3D pets that hang out on your taskbar while you work, play, or do nothing at all. There's no window to keep open. They walk, nap, pull tricks, and get a little grumpy if you ignore them for too long.

**Four characters, one free.** Ping the snoop hears everything and starts the gossip. Tutu the diva faints when you walk away. Moss the slacker lies face down and calls it a plan. Rocco the menace steals your cursor and judges your typing. Pick one, keep the name or give it yours.

**They know you.** Tell them about yourself in the Notebook. They remember, bring it up later, and gossip about you to each other at a little round table. No account, no cloud, nothing leaves your computer.

**Attitude, your call.** Sweet, Cheeky or Menace. Menace steals your cursor for a second, leaves muddy footprints, spins out, and lies down right where you're working. Never breaks anything.

**A house on your taskbar.** Two styles. Click a room to decorate it. They nap in the bedroom, eat in the kitchen, and take bathroom breaks behind a curtain, which is all anyone sees.

**More pets, more chaos.** With more than one out they dance, chase, wrestle, race, swap hats, and hold a parade. Play with a pet on seven different days and it finds an egg that hatches into a new one in a rolled colour. Eggs are earned, never sold.

**Dress them up.** A closet of hats and glasses, a gold chain, a hoodie, and a hat maker: pick a shape, two colours and a sticker, name it, and send the code to a friend so their pet can wear it too.

**Made for clips.** One click records eight seconds of your pet on your real desktop as a GIF. A green-screen stage window with buttons for streamers.

No subscription, no timers, no prize boxes.
