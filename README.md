# Perchlings

Cute 3D pets that live on your desktop. You buy one, download it, click it, and it hops out and wanders your screen. Close the window; the pet stays. Name it, pick five things it can do, dress it from its closet. It learns your routine, celebrates birthdays, and sulks when you ignore it.

This repo is the whole product: the pets' look, the tools that turn that look into game-ready art, the desktop app, and the shop site.

## Layout

```
web/pets.js                 The pets and their closet items, as three.js geometry. Single source of truth for the look.
web/look-test.html          Interactive demo (drag to turn, click a pet, pick a mood and a hat). Loads pets.js.
web/dist/look-test.html     Same demo with pets.js inlined, for publishing. Built by tools/build_looktest.py.
tools/sprites/sprite.html   Renders one pet, one mood, one pose, one angle, transparent background.
tools/sprites/render_sprites.py   Drives headless Chrome over every pet/mood/pose/angle and packs sprite sheets.
assets/sprites/<pet>/       Rendered frames + <pet>_sheet.png + <pet>_sheet.json (generated, not hand-edited).
assets/sprites/<pet>/outfits/   One sheet per closet item, rendered as a see-through layer that fits that pet.
docs/                       Look recipe, catalog, decisions, house style for words.
app/perchling.py            The Windows desktop app: one pet, always on top, wandering the taskbar.
app/species/<pet>.json      What each pet can do: its catalog of tricks and behaviours, and its five starting picks.
app/closet.json             The closet: shelves and items, by id. Geometry for each item is in web/pets.js.
Perchling.bat               Double-click to start your adopted pets (first time: the adoption window). "Perchling.bat horns" starts Gold.
tools/build_exe.py          Builds the download: dist/Perchlings/Perchlings.exe and a zip. Nothing in dist/ or build/ is committed.
site/                       The shop site, plain HTML. Published to GitHub Pages by .github/workflows/pages.yml.
site/pets.js, site/img/     Generated by tools/build_site.py from web/pets.js and the sprite sheets. Do not edit by hand.
```

## The four pets

Labelled by colour until they have names. Each keeps one silhouette feature.

| Label | Feature | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Teal | antenna with a ball | cute (baby proportions, blush) |
| Pink | round side ears | cute |
| Green | leaf on top | calm (a little older) |
| Gold | small horns | cool (smaller eyes set higher, half-lidded, no blush, taller) |

Moods: happy, surprised, sleepy, sulky. Poses: idle, blink, walk1, walk2, squash, stretch, sit, lie, wave1, wave2, study, work, game, eat1, eat2. Tricks so far: bounce, peekaboo, zoomies, nap anywhere, sit, lie down, spin, wave. Habits: calm, sleepy, clingy, show-off. Together: study, work, game, eat with me (started from the menu; the pet sits down with the right props and stays until the owner clicks it). Every pet takes a bathroom or shower break now and then (a curtain drawn in Pillow drops in front of it for 15-25 s, a paper roll or soap bubbles say which; "Occupied." if clicked; out with a stretch and "Don't ask." or "Fresh."), and every pet has Hide (turns into a plain folder, peeks over the edge now and then, jumps out when clicked) and Remind me (day, time, what for; delivered as a hop and a bubble that stays until clicked, or on the next arrival if the computer was off).

## Run things

Needs Python 3.12 with Pillow, and Chrome or Edge installed. No admin rights needed.

```
python app/perchling.py --pet antenna               # put Teal on the desktop (ears, leaf, horns for the others)
python tools/build_looktest.py                      # rebuild web/dist/look-test.html
python tools/sprites/render_sprites.py --pets antenna --moods happy,surprised,sleepy,sulky --poses idle,blink,walk1,walk2,squash,stretch --yaws 0,60,300 --shadow 0
python tools/sprites/render_sprites.py --pets antenna --layers beanie,crown --poses idle,walk1,walk2,squash,stretch --yaws 0,60,300 --shadow 0
python tools/sprites/render_sprites.py --pets leaf --layers beanie --yaws 0 --keep     # pet and hat together, to check the fit
```

Open `web/dist/look-test.html` in a browser to see the pets.

## How the desktop app draws them

Real-time 3D on the desktop is v2. v1 pre-renders the same three.js pets to transparent sprite frames (this repo's `tools/sprites`) and plays them in a small always-on-top window, the same way Beam works. At pet size it looks the same as live 3D and costs almost nothing to run.

Start it with no arguments and it opens the adoption window the first time (which pet, its name, its 5 picks), then starts every adopted pet after that. Right-click the pet for its menu: Tickle, Tricks, Together, Hide, Remind me, Pick five, Closet, Shop (everything it can do or wear, one line each, with pictures; prices land here when extras exist), Rename, Set your birthday, Start with Windows, Quit. "Start with Windows" writes one small .cmd file into the owner's own Startup folder, so the pet is there at every login without admin rights. What it learns (login times, attention, what it wears) is saved in `%APPDATA%\Perchlings\<pet>.json` and nowhere else.

Lonely means: no cursor on the pet for 1 hour; from then on it hops and asks "Play with me?" every 5 to 7 minutes. Ignored means: no cursor on the pet for 2 hours. Hovering, clicking, dragging, tickling and opening its menu all count as touching (`last_touch` in the state file). After 2 hours it sulks (back turned, "Hmph.", a glance over the shoulder now and then) and keeps sulking until touched; a tickle forgives it at once. Everything it does on its own (tricks, sitting, breaks, the activities it starts) carries on regardless.

Monitors: drag the pet onto any screen and let go; it falls to that screen's taskbar and remembers the screen for next time (`mon` in the state file).

## The closet

Items are built in `web/pets.js` next to the pets, hung off the head so they follow every pose, and seated per pet so the pet's own feature stays in view (a hat sits to one side on Teal so the antenna is clear, to the other side on Green for the leaf). Each item is rendered as its own sprite sheet per pet, with the pet blocking the view where it should but painting nothing. The app lays the item frame over the pet frame, so one pet sheet works with any number of items.

Hats first, because they are the shelf people buy most (`docs/catalog.md`): beanie, crown, party hat, chef hat, mushroom cap, flower crown. Then the face shelf (glasses, sunglasses) and headphones. One item per shelf can be worn at once, and they stack: glasses under headphones under a hat. Shelves still to come, in order of evidence: neck, body, back, glow, colours.

## The download

```
python tools/build_exe.py                           # dist/Perchlings/ and dist/Perchlings-<version>-windows.zip
dist\Perchlings\Perchlings.exe --pet antenna --selftest
```

`tools/installer.iss` turns that folder into `PerchlingsSetup-<version>.exe` with Inno Setup, and into one flavour per pet (`-Teal`, `-Pink`, `-Green`, `-Gold`) that leaves a `pet.txt` next to the exe so the bought pet walks out with no questions asked; the plain one asks. Details: installs for the current user only (no admin password), desktop icon and Start menu entry, optional "start with Windows", shows up in Add or remove programs, and opens the pet when it finishes. GitHub's Windows machine has Inno Setup already, so the release carries both the installer and the zip.

GitHub builds it too: `.github/workflows/build-download.yml` runs the same script on a Windows machine at GitHub every time `app/`, the sheets or the build script change, and attaches the zip to a release named after `VERSION` (https://github.com/Pinkpelara/perchling/releases). The local build is for testing before a push.

One folder, one exe, about 39 MB zipped. It ships the app, the species packs, the closet and the packed sprite sheets, nothing else. The zip also carries a short READ ME FIRST.txt. It is not code-signed yet, so Windows shows its "unknown app" warning on first run; the Microsoft Store listing is what removes that. The version number lives in `app/perchling.py` (`VERSION`) and shows at the bottom of the pet's menu.

## The notebook

`app/petnotes.py`. The owner types notes into the pet (menu: Notebook...; `notes` in the state file, up to 300 of 240 characters). The pet reacts on save, brings a note up every 15 to 35 minutes when things are calm (sooner right after a new note), mentions the latest one on arrival half the time, and passes names and likes on in gossip. Recall is pattern matching, nothing more: a named person or pet ("How's Max?"), likes and dislikes, a favorite, a feeling ("Better today?"), an event (before: "Ready for the exam?"; two days on: "How did the exam go?"), something said about the pet ("You said I'm shy."), a plan ("You're learning to play guitar, right?"); anything else comes back as "You told me yesterday: ...". Never sent anywhere. The owner's name and pronouns ("my name is Polin", "call me Sam", "I'm a woman", "she/her", "they/them") are learned from any pet's notebook and kept in `%APPDATA%\Perchlings\owner.json` for the whole household; lines to the owner then carry the name ("Right on time, Polin.") and gossip uses the name and pronouns, every other line.

## What they say at the table

`app/pettalk.py`. The pet that calls a gossip reads every pet's file (`household_states()`), pulls the facts (`facts()`: people and pets by name, likes, dislikes, favorites, feelings, events with "on Friday" turned into days from now, plans, what the owner said about each pet, picks, days since adoption, hours since each pet was touched, birthday and days until it, the usual arrival time and whether today was early or late, the next reminder) and writes one conversation (`conversation()`): up to six short exchanges that follow on from each other ("Polin has an interview coming up." / "In 4 days." / "Let's be quiet that morning."), lines about a particular pet said by that pet, seats rotating otherwise. The whole script travels in the plan file, so every pet at the table follows the same one. There is no filler; a household with nothing written down talks about arrival times, who was touched last, picks, and the fact that nobody knows the owner's name yet.

## Two pets on one desktop

`app/household.py`. Each pet writes where it is to `%APPDATA%\Perchlings\household\here\<pet>.json` a few times a second and reads the others. Now and then (every 4 to 12 minutes, when both are idle on the same screen) one writes a plan (`plans\<a>-<b>.json`: kind, shared start time, meeting point, seed); the other picks it up within half a second, and both build their own side of the same script from the same seed, so they stay in step without talking again. Pair kinds: dance, chase, wrestle, race, nap, copycat, hatswap, peekaboo, gossip. With three or more out, a plan can include everyone (`group`, slots in a line): dance, race, nap, peekaboo, parade (lines come from what each pet knows: usual login time, how long since a touch, birthday, the other's hat). A pet that comes out gets a wave. The owner can ask for any of these from the menu ("Play with the others"; the nearest pet, or everyone for the group kinds). After a play everyone walks off in a different direction, and idle walks are sometimes long strolls, so they don't end up clumped. Nothing leaves the computer.

## Music, reactions, mischief, signs, photos, parties

`app/fun.py`. `AudioEar` reads the speakers' peak level ten times a second (pycaw + comtypes, Core Audio meter; no audio content, just loudness) and sets `music` after 2.5 s of sound; a pet that is idle then dances (squash/stretch on a beat, turning left and right, a small hop) until it's been quiet for 4 s. `KeyWatch` polls key states with GetAsyncKeyState and keeps only timestamps: typing rate, click rate, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+S runs; never which keys. Reactions (menu toggle "Reacts to you"): typing ≥5 keys/s → a hop and "Go go go." (once per 10 min), 3 undos in 6 s → "Oops.", 3 saves → "Saved. Again.", ≥4 clicks/s → "Easy.", the lock screen (OpenInputDesktop fails) → sleep until unlocked, then "Welcome back.", midnight → a yawn. Mischief is a habit pick: every 8 to 20 minutes one of: hold the cursor on the pet for 1.6 s ("Mine."), a walk that leaves muddy footprints (small colour-keyed windows that vanish after 9 s), or dragging a sticky note ("be nice to Pip") that stays for 30 s. "Hold a sign..." draws the words on a board the pet holds for 30 s or until clicked. "Photo..." saves a framed card with stickers to Pictures\Perchlings and opens the folder. Parties: on a birthday or adoption day, confetti (an 18-frame overlay) and a party hat for 3 minutes; `household/party.json` gets every other pet to join.

AudioEar judges sound over a window (music on when 60% of the last three seconds had level above 0.008, off after four seconds with almost none), because music at low volume dips under any bar between beats; the menu shows "hearing sound now" / "it's quiet" / "can't hear the speakers" next to the checkbox. KeyWatch (`fun.py`) is a thread polling `GetAsyncKeyState` for the typing keys every 30 ms while the keyboard is busy (100 ms when quiet), counting key-down moments only. Polling every 100 ms from the tick saw a third of real presses (a tap lasts 40 to 80 ms), and the "pressed since last call" flag is shared between processes, so pets would eat each other's presses. Bars: 4.5 keys/s over four seconds for the cheer, three clicks/s for "Easy.", three Ctrl+Z or Ctrl+S within six seconds.

## Not just for kids

Closet shelves neck (gold chain) and body (hoodie; body items hang off `bodyRoot`, not the head, and get their own layers per pose), hats snapback (included) and bucket hat. Poses dab and flex; tricks dab, flex, moonwalk (walk frames facing one way while sliding the other). The dance cycles bob, moonwalk, bob, spin, bob, dab-and-flex, two seconds each. The house has two styles rendered from the same geometry (`STYLES` in house.js, `?style=loft`): Cozy (gable roof, cream) and Loft (flat roof with a rail and a planter, charcoal, black frames, wide windows, orange door), chosen in Decorate; wall choices gained charcoal, navy, sage and terracotta, and Loft defaults to them.

## The hat maker
`app/hatmaker.py`, menu "Hat maker..." (and a button in the closet). Six shapes live in `MAKER` in `web/pets.js`, built with id colours: the main part red, the trim green, the sticker spot blue (half strength, and `sprite.html?flat=1` turns tone mapping off so the channels stay apart). `tools/sprites/render_maker.py` renders them as layers `outfits/maker_<shape>_sheet.png` per pet, same poses as the closet items. In the app `HM.paint()` recolours a frame by channel (the shared minimum of the channels is the shine and stays), and draws the sticker on the spot, shaded by the blue channel. Hats are `%APPDATA%\Perchlings\hats\<id>.json` (id = hash of shape, colours, sticker), worn as `wearing.hat = "my:<id>"`; every pet, the house and the stage paint them the same way. The code `PH1-<shape>-<main>-<trim>-<sticker>-<sticker colour>-<name>` is the whole hat; `from_code()` accepts it. On the web, `wear(THREE, pet, sp, "maker:cap:1E1B24:F4F1EA")` builds a shape in real colours (the look test has a Make row).

## Pet ids, eggs and colours

A pet id is its species id, or species id plus `#n` for a hatchling (`ears#2`); `species_of()` strips the suffix, state files are named by pet id, and `adopted_ids()` lists the four kinds first then hatchlings. `app/eggs.py`: the odds table (common 60% natural colour, uncommon 30% Lilac/Mint/Peach/Sky, rare 9% Midnight/Sunset/Snow, legendary 1% Golden), `roll()`, `recolor()` (HSV: hue shifted from the species' base hue to the colour's, saturation and lightness scaled; applied to the pet's own frames in `Frames.compose`, so outfits are untouched), `egg_image()`. A pet counts a "good day" when touched 3+ times (`care` in its state); after 7 good days since `egg_baseline` it finds an egg (`household/egg.json`, one per household, at most 6 pets), which sits next to it (an overlay that follows it; click for the time left) and hatches 24 h later: a new state file `<species>#n` with `hatched: true`, `variant`, a name like "Peach Teal", started as its own process. Hatchlings are drawn at 72% for a week, 86% the second week. Presence carries `species` and `variant`, so the house and the stage draw the right colour.

## The streamer stage

`app/stage.py` (`Perchlings.exe --stage`, from any pet's menu). A normal window titled "Perchlings Stage" with a chroma backdrop (green, blue, magenta, or cream/night for non-keyed use) that draws every pet that is out, at its relative taskbar position, from what each pet announces (mood, pose, yaw, wearing, and the current speech bubble, which presence now carries), plus the closed house where it stands. Buttons write `household/commands/<pet>.json` (tickle, wave, dance for 20 s, gossip and party to one pet who brings the others in, everyone out of the house); pets pick commands up in `mind_others`. OBS: Window Capture the stage, add a Chroma Key filter.

## The house

`web/house.js` is the house as three.js geometry (a two-storey cross-section: living room and kitchen down, bedroom and bathroom up, a staircase, a gable roof; plus a closed outside with a door and windows) and fourteen pieces of furniture, each placed in its room. `tools/sprites/house.html` + `render_house.py` render `assets/house/closed.png` (512, for the taskbar), `open.png` (1200 x 900, walls neutral), one see-through layer per piece in `furniture/`, and `layout.json` (per room: floor line, where a pet stands, how tall a pet is there, the box to paint; plus the door and ground in both views). Walls are painted in the app by multiplying the room box with the chosen colour.

`app/house.py` is its own program (`Perchlings.exe --house`, started by any pet that finds no house out; one instance). Closed, it is a colour-keyed window on the taskbar, draggable across monitors like a pet, right-click for Open, Decorate, Come out, Quit. A click opens it: the painted open render plus the furniture that is on, then every pet whose presence file says `inside: <room>` drawn from its own sheets at that room's spot (bedroom asleep on the bed, living room sitting on the couch, kitchen eating, bathroom behind the curtain, which is drawn last). It writes `household/house.json` (door position, monitor, open) a few times a second so pets know where the door is. State in `%APPDATA%\Perchlings\house.json` (position, furniture on/off, wall colours); shop pieces are `house:<piece>` in `owned.json`.

Pets: `go_inside(room, seconds)` walks to the door then hides the window and announces the room; `come_out()` reappears at the door and walks off; the house's Come out menu writes `plans/house-out-<pet>.json`. Breaks happen in the bathroom when a house is out; six in ten naps go to the bedroom for 2 to 5 minutes; the menu has Go inside (living room, bedroom, kitchen, up to 15 minutes).

The open house: a left click on a room opens that room's panel (its pieces and wall colours, `room_dialog`, placed at the click; `room_at` hit-tests `LAYOUT["rooms"][*]["wall"]` down to the floor), a drag moves the whole open house along the taskbar (`open_drag`, `open_pos`), and only the right-click menu closes it. Decorate everything at once from the menu; the style switch is there too.

## Owning things

`app/closet.json` marks what comes with every pet (`included`: beanie, party hat, glasses). `app/shop.json` lists the rest with prices, the store link, the store's license endpoint, and `variants`: a map from the store's variant id to the item ids it unlocks (filled in once the products exist). Purchases live in `%APPDATA%\Perchlings\owned.json`, shared by every pet in the household. The Shop window shows Buy buttons (open the store in the browser) and the menu has "Enter a code..." which checks the code once with the store and unlocks the items. Before the store exists, a local `test-codes.json` next to `owned.json` maps test codes to items; it is never shipped.

Households: every adopted pet comes out with the desktop icon unless it is "at home" (`home` in its state file). The Pets submenu on any pet sends others home or brings them out, and "Adopt another..." opens the adoption window (`--adopt`).

## Updates

The packaged app asks GitHub for the newest release 20 s after it starts and every 6 h after that (`newest_version()`, the public releases API, nothing sent but the version number in the user agent). When there is a newer one, the pet says so and an "Update to x.y.z" item appears at the bottom of the menu. `do_update()` downloads `PerchlingsSetup.exe` into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Perchlings-update` and runs it with `/SILENT /FORCECLOSEAPPLICATIONS /NORESTART`; the installer closes every running pet, replaces the files, and its silent-mode `[Run]` entry starts `Perchlings.exe` again, which brings out everyone who isn't at home. The per-pet `pet.txt` survives, and so do all the state files.

## The site

`site/index.html` is the whole shop site: what a Perchling is, the four pets, a live 3D lineup (click a pet to try a hat on it), how it works, what it does, the price, questions. It uses the same `pets.js` as the app, so the pets on the site are the pets you get.

Two lines to fill in before launch, at the top of `site/site.js`: `buyUrl` (the checkout link from the payment provider) and `contact` (an email address for the footer). Until `buyUrl` is set, the buttons read "Adoptions open soon".

It lives at https://pinkpelara.github.io/perchling/ (the playground page is at /playground.html). Every push to `main` that touches `site/` publishes it through GitHub Pages; run `python tools/build_site.py` first when the pets or the closet changed. Nothing runs on a local server.

## The test run
`python tools/testrun.py` drives everything on the real app code in a scratch data folder (`.testrun/`, wiped when all is well): one pet in-process through every trick, together pick, hide, breaks, the sulk and the nudge, reminders, the notebook, a sign, a photo, a party, the dance, reactions, mischief, the house, eggs and hatching, the hat maker and every dialog, with every frame it asks for checked against the sheet; then the other three pets; then real pets, the house and the stage as separate programs, driven through the command files (`household/commands/<pid>.json`: tickle, wave, dance, gossip, party, out, and `play`/`trick`/`together`/`inside`/`hide`/`break`/`wear` with their fields). `quick` runs the first part only, `part2` the processes only. Run it before a release; it takes about ten minutes and the pets appear on the taskbar while it runs.

## Rules

- The catalog (tricks, behaviours, gadgets, closet shelves) is research-backed; every item has a source. See `docs/catalog.md`.
- No wellness or productivity features.
- No ads, no mystery boxes, no chat, no social feed. Fixed prices.
- Local-first: what the pet learns about the owner stays on their computer.
- An adult holds the account; a child can be the owner. No birth years, location, photos or messaging.
