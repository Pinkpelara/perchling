# Catalog

What a Perchling can do, and where each item comes from. Every line points at a real product where people already use or pay for it. New items need the same "why" column before they go in.

Sources checked (September 2026): Shimeji (2009, still shared every year), Desktop Goose (2020), Desktop Mate and Bongo Cat (2025 paid and free hits), the Steam desktop-pet wave of 2025–26, Adopt Me and Nintendogs (what kids expect a pet to learn), Roblox avatar trends 2026.

## Three findings that shape the list

1. The favourite behaviours haven't changed in sixteen years. Shimeji in 2009 and Desktop Mate in 2025 sell the same things: follow the cursor, climb and sit on windows, nap on the taskbar, get picked up and thrown, react to touch. These ship with every pet. They are not picks.
2. Outfits sell better than gadgets, everywhere. Bongo Cat's shop is hats, skins and emotes. Roblox's 2026 trends are headphones, glasses, halos and glowing effects. Desktop Mate sells characters and motions. Nobody makes money on props, so launch with four gadgets and let owners ask for more.
3. Kids expect the tricks they already know (Nintendogs, Adopt Me): sit, lie down, beg, roll over, jump, dance, play dead, handstand, backflip. Adults made Bongo Cat huge because it reacts when they type and stays above the taskbar without getting in the way.

## Picks: behaviours (personality)

Shimeji ships a calm build and a naughty build because owners split that way. Desktop Mate's forum asks for "Cute and Helpful" and "Mischief and Needy" by name.

| Behaviour | Why |
|---|---|
| Calm | Shimeji's calm build; what adults choose at work |
| Mischief (harmless: hides your cursor for a second, drags only its own note window) | Shimeji's naughty build; Desktop Goose |
| Sleepy | Desktop Mate on the taskbar; Shimeji asleep |
| Clingy | Requested on Desktop Mate's forum; follows you window to window, sulks louder |
| Shy | Hides behind windows, peeks out |
| Show-off | Does tricks unprompted; Adopt Me pet displays |
| Leaves notes and doodles | Desktop Goose's notes, the thing everyone screenshotted |
| Dances when music plays | Desktop Mate |
| Plays with your other pet | Desktop Pets, Desktop Mate multi-character; the reason to buy pet two |

## Left out on purpose

Multiplying (chaos, gets uninstalled). Grabbing or throwing the owner's real windows (funny once). Prize boxes with tiny odds (every price is printed). AI chat (a pet that talks about itself stops feeling like a pet).
