# Look recipe

What makes a Perchling read as cute, and how the family gets range. This is the brief for anyone touching `web/pets.js` or modelling a new pet.

## Shared body language

- Round vinyl-toy bodies with a clearcoat shine. Smooth, never blocky.
- Head heavier than the body.
- Big eyes with two highlights each: a large one upper-outer, a small one lower-inner.
- A real mouth. Smile at rest; open mouth with a tongue when happy; a small "o" when surprised or asleep; a frown when sulking.
- Cheek blush.
- Thin eyebrows that carry the mood: lifted for happy, high for surprised, low for sleepy, angled in for sulky.
- Stubby feet close to the body, little arm nubs, a lighter belly patch.
- One silhouette feature per pet that reads from across the room.

## Three styles, one family

| | cute | calm | cool |
|---|---|---|---|
| eye size | 100% | 88% | 76% |
| eye height on face | low | slightly higher | high |
| blush | strong | faint | none |
| eyebrow movement | full | 80% | 45% |
| mouth size | full | 85% | 70% |
| half-lidded eyes | no | no | yes |
| body height | short | medium | tall |
| head size | largest | large | smaller |

Teal and Pink are cute. Green is calm. Gold is cool. Later pets should pick a style so the family keeps its range and adults have pets that don't feel childish on a work desk.

## Colour

Saturated, not pastel. Rendered with ACES tone mapping at exposure 0.92 and a low ambient light so the colours stay deep.

| Pet | body | belly | shade | accent |

| Teal | #2FB3A3 | #BFE8E0 | #1F7F73 | #7EDCD0 |
| Pink | #EE8FA4 | #F9D0D9 | #C46A7E | #F3ADBD |
| Green | #58A64E | #C6E0B4 | #356F2A | #7CC46A |
| Gold | #E0862A | #F4D2A0 | #A85F16 | #F2B85A |

Eyes #1E1B24, mouth #3B2733, blush #F58EA6, tongue #F27C8F, brows #2A2230.

## Motion (idle)

Breathe (slow scale), bob (slow vertical), blink every 2.5 to 6 seconds, head follows the cursor within about 16 degrees. Sleepy pets don't follow the cursor. Sulky pets turn their head away about 30 degrees and drop it slightly.
