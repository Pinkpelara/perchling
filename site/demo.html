Four characters. Pick one, or collect them.

PingThe snoop

Hears everything, tells everyone. Startles easily, forgets nothing, and starts most of the gossip.

Signature move: Peekaboo. Says things like Hi. Did you hear that? You forgot me. Mine.

TutuThe diva

Big ears, bigger reactions. Faints when you leave, poses when you're back, and expects applause.

Signature move: Faint. Says things like Hi. Look at me. Hmph. Mine.

MossThe slacker

Lies face down and calls it a plan. Slow to move, quick to nap, oddly wise about it.

Signature move: Rot. Says things like Hi. ... Mine.

RoccoThe menace

Half-lidded, fully unbothered. Steals your cursor, judges your typing, and thinks it's famous. It might be.

Signature move: Side-eye. Says things like Sup. Hmph. Mine.

Every pet can be renamed. The names are just how they arrive.
