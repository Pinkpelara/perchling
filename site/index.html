How it works

Adopt one

Pay once and you get a link to a small app for Windows. No account, no sign-up.

Open it

Your pet pops up at the bottom of your screen and starts wandering around. Right-click it to name it, pick its 5 things, or open the closet.

Hang out

There's no window to keep open. Turn on Start with Windows and your pet is there waiting every morning.

What it does

  • Hangs out on your taskbar and wanders around. Turns back when it reaches the edge of the screen.
  • Pick 5. Choose 5 from its list: tricks like bounce, peekaboo, zoomies, sit, lie down, spin, wave, and nap anywhere, plus habits like calm, sleepy, clingy, and show-off. Swap them anytime from the menu. Want more? Extras are $0.99 to $2.99 each.
  • Dress it up. The closet has hats, glasses, and headphones to start, and more shelves are on the way. Whatever it's wearing stays on until you change it.
  • Pick it up. Drag it anywhere and let go. It lands with a little squish. Click it for a tickle.
  • Learns your routine. It figures out when you usually show up. Log in at your usual time and it lets you know it noticed.
  • Birthdays. Tell it yours (just the day and month) and it bounces around to celebrate. It celebrates its own adoption day too.
  • Gets grumpy. Ignore it long enough and it turns its back on you with a "Hmph." One tickle and you're friends again.
  • Never dies, never nags, never sends you a notification.

Questions

Do I have to keep the app open?

Nope. Your pet is the app, so there's no other window to keep open. When you want it gone, right-click it and choose Quit.

What does it know about me?

When you usually show up, your birthday if you told it (day and month only), what it's wearing, and how much you've played with it. All of that lives in one small file on your computer. Nothing gets sent anywhere. No account, no email, no tracking.

Can my kid have one?

Yes. A grown-up buys it, and the kid can be the owner. There's no account for kids, no chat, no messages, and nothing to buy inside the pet without the grown-up.

What about Mac? Phones?

Windows 10 and 11 today. Mac is next. Phones don't let a pet walk on top of other apps, so we're not promising one.

Will Windows warn me when I open it?

Maybe, for now. Until the pet is in the Microsoft Store, Windows can show a warning for an app it hasn't seen before. Click More info, then Run anyway. The app only touches its own folder and your pet's one small file.

What if it's not for me?

Write to us within 14 days and you get your money back. No questions asked.
