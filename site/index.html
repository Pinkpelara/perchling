What they do

Right-click your pet and it's all in one menu.

It lives on your taskbar.

It hangs out down there while you work. It'll pull a trick when it feels like it, sit down for a bit, and say hi when you come back.

Tricks on command.

Backflip, moonwalk, karate, robot, parkour, yoga, panic, scream, jumpscare and a lot more. Pick one from the menu, or let it surprise you. Each pet has its own favorite.

Play music and they dance.

Turn on any song and every pet on your screen dances in step.

Dress it up.

Crowns, hoodies, sunglasses, headphones, angel wings, a jetpack. The closet shows your pet wearing it before you pick. Or make your own hat in the hat maker and send a friend the code.

Build its house.

A little house sits on your taskbar with four rooms to decorate, in two styles. Your pet naps in the bedroom, eats in the kitchen and takes bathroom breaks.

Pick its attitude.

Sweet, Cheeky or Menace. Cheeky leaves muddy footprints and sticky notes. Menace steals your cursor, spins out and lies down on your work.

Hide it as a folder.

Pick Hide and it turns into a plain folder. Eyes peek out now and then. Pick Come out and it hops back.

Tell it things.

Tell it your cat's name, or that you've got a test Friday. It remembers and brings it up later. Get two pets and they sit down and gossip about you.

Get two and they play.

They chase each other, race, wrestle, swap hats and parade across your screen.

Hatch eggs.

Play with your pet on seven different days and it finds an egg. A day later a mini hatches in one of nine colors, from Mint to Golden, and stays tiny for good.

Study, work or game with you.

Pick one and your pet pulls out a laptop, headphones or a snack and settles in next to you until you click it.

It notices you.

Type fast and it cheers you on. Hit undo three times in a row and you'll get a comment. Lock your computer and it naps till you're back.

It learns your day.

After a few days it knows when you usually sit down and says hi on time. Leave it alone for two hours and it sulks till you tickle it. Tell it your birthday and you get confetti on the day.

Questions

Do I have to keep something open?

No. The pet is the app. Right-click it and pick Quit when you want a break, and uninstall it like any other app.

Windows warned me about the file.

Windows does that with new apps it hasn't seen much yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Will it get in my way?

It sticks to the taskbar and stays out of your windows. If you want it gone for a bit, Hide turns it into a folder, and Sweet keeps it off your cursor.

What does it know about me?

Only what you type into its notebook and when you usually show up, and that stays on your computer. It can't read what you type or hear what song is on.

I have two screens.

Drag it onto either one. It remembers where it lives.
