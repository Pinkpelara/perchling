What it does

  • Hangs out on your taskbar and wanders around. Turns back when it reaches the edge of the screen.
  • Pick 5. Choose 5 from its list: tricks like bounce, peekaboo, zoomies, sit, lie down, spin, wave, and nap anywhere; habits like calm, sleepy, clingy, and show-off; and Together picks. Swap them anytime from the menu. Want more? Extras are $0.99 to $2.99 each.
  • Together. Study with me, work with me, game with me, eat with me. Pick one and your pet sits down next to you with a laptop and a book, a laptop and a mug, a controller and headphones, or a bowl of food, and stays until you click it.
  • Hide. Tell it to hide and it turns into a folder on your desktop. Every so often two eyes peek over the edge. Click the folder and it jumps out.
  • More pets. With more than one on the same screen they dance, chase, wrestle (nobody gets hurt), race, nap side by side, copy each other's tricks, swap hats, play peekaboo, and gossip about you. Three or more and the whole gang dances in a line, races, naps in a pile, and parades after a leader. They wave when someone new shows up.
  • Breaks. Every so often it says "Be right back", a shower curtain drops in front of it, and all you see is the curtain sway, or soap bubbles floating up if it's a shower. Then it comes out with a stretch. A meal is always followed by one, a few minutes later. What happens behind the curtain stays behind the curtain.
  • Notebook. Tell it about yourself, any day: who's in your life, what you like, how today went. It remembers and brings things up later in its own words. "My dog is called Max" comes back as "How's Max?" A tired day comes back as "Better today?" Tell it your name and it uses it. And when your pets gossip, it's about what they actually know: your usual arrival time, your birthday, Max. No account and no AI in the cloud; your notes never leave your computer.
  • Remind me. Type a day, a time and what for. At that moment it hops and holds up your note until you click it. If the computer was off, it tells you the moment it sees you again.
  • Dress it up. A beanie, a party hat and glasses come with your pet. The shop has more: a crown, a chef hat, a mushroom cap, a flower crown, sunglasses, headphones, $1.49 to $1.99 each, yours forever. Whatever it's wearing stays on until you change it.
  • Pick it up. Drag it anywhere and let go. It lands with a little squish. Click it for a tickle.
  • Learns your routine. It figures out when you usually show up. Log in at your usual time and it lets you know it noticed.
  • Birthdays. Tell it yours (just the day and month) and it bounces around to celebrate. It celebrates its own adoption day too.
  • Gets grumpy. Ignore it long enough and it turns its back on you with a "Hmph." One tickle and you're friends again.
  • Never dies, never nags, never sends you a notification.

Questions

Do I have to keep the app open?

Nope. Your pet is the app, so there's no other window to keep open. When you want it gone for now, right-click it and choose Quit. To remove it completely, use "Add or remove programs" like any other app.

What does it know about me?

When you usually show up, your birthday if you told it (day and month only), what it's wearing, and how much you've played with it. All of that lives in one small file on your computer. The only time the app talks to the internet is when you type in a purchase code, to check it once with the store. No account, no email, no tracking.

Can my kid have one?

Yes. A grown-up buys it, and the kid can be the owner. There's no account for kids, no chat, no messages, and nothing to buy inside the pet without the grown-up.

What about Mac? Phones?

Windows 10 and 11 today. Mac is next. Phones don't let a pet walk on top of other apps, so we're not promising one.

Will Windows warn me when I open it?

Maybe, for now. Until the pet is in the Microsoft Store, Windows can show a warning for an app it hasn't seen before. Click More info, then Run anyway. The app installs into your own user folder, needs no admin password, and shows up in "Add or remove programs" like anything else.

What if it's not for me?

Write to us within 14 days and you get your money back. No questions asked.
