What they do

The clips are made from the app's own pets and moves, so this is what you get. Right-click your pet and all of it is in one menu.

It does its own thing.

It hangs out on the taskbar, pulls a trick when it feels like it, and sits down for a bit. It notices when you leave and says hi when you're back.

Play music and it dances.

Play music in any app and a couple of seconds later it starts dancing. It only hears how loud your speakers are, not what's playing.

How much trouble it makes.

Out of the box it's Cheeky: muddy footprints and a sticky note now and then. Turn it up to Menace and it steals your cursor and spins out. Sweet means none of that.

It turns into a folder.

Pick Hide and it's a plain folder. Drag it along the taskbar or onto another screen and it stays one. Every so often two eyes peek over the edge. Pick Come out and it hops back.

A little house on the taskbar.

Your pet walks in for naps and bathroom breaks. Click the house and it opens up, and you decorate the rooms.

Tell it things.

Type your cat's name into its notebook and it brings it up later. If you have two, they sit down together and gossip about you. All of it stays on your computer.

If you get two.

They chase each other and swap hats. Even with one, play with it on seven different days and it finds an egg. A day later a mini hatches, in a random color, and stays small for good.

It notices your typing.

It cheers when you type fast and has a comment when you undo three times. It keeps the timing of your key presses and a note when you hit undo or save, nothing else, so it can't read a word you write.

Clips and streams.

Pick Clip and it records eight seconds of itself as a GIF you can post. If you stream, there's a green screen window with every pet on it.

Questions

Do I have to keep something open?

No. The pet is the app, so there's no window behind it. Right-click it and pick Quit when you want it gone for a while. Uninstall it like any other app to remove it for good.

What does it know about me?

It knows what you typed into its notebook, roughly when you sit down each day, and your birthday if you give it one. That stays in a small folder on your computer. It only goes online for updates and to confirm your code once.

Will it get in my way?

It keeps to the taskbar and stays out of your windows. If you need it gone for a bit, Hide turns it into a folder. Set it to Sweet and it never touches your cursor.

I have two screens.

Drag it onto whichever one you like and it stays there. Each pet remembers its own spot.

Does it work on a Mac or a phone?

It runs on Windows 10 and 11 for now. There won't be a phone version.
