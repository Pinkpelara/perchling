Questions

Do I have to keep the app open?

Nope. Your pet is the app, so there's no other window to keep open. When you want it gone for now, right-click it and choose Quit. To remove it completely, use "Add or remove programs" like any other app.

What does it know about me?

When you usually show up, your birthday if you told it (day and month only), what it's wearing, and how much you've played with it. All of that lives in one small file on your computer. The app talks to the internet for two things only: once a day it asks whether there's a newer version (and updates itself when you say so), and when you type in a purchase code it checks it once with the store. Nothing about you goes with either. No account, no email, no tracking.

Can my kid have one?

Yes. A grown-up buys it, and the kid can be the owner. There's no account for kids, no chat, no messages, and nothing to buy inside the pet without the grown-up.

What about Mac? Phones?

Windows 10 and 11 today. Mac is next. Phones don't let a pet walk on top of other apps, so we're not promising one.

Will Windows warn me when I open it?

Maybe, for now. Until the pet is in the Microsoft Store, Windows can show a warning for an app it hasn't seen before. Click More info, then Run anyway. The app installs into your own user folder, needs no admin password, and shows up in "Add or remove programs" like anything else.

How do I add another pet later?

Adopt it on the site like the first one. You get a code; right-click any pet, choose Enter a code, and the new one walks out. Same for closet items, effects and house pieces. New computer? Install the household there and enter your codes again.

What if it's not for me?

Write to us within 14 days and you get your money back. No questions asked.
