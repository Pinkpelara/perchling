How it works

Buy it

You get a link to one small program for Windows. No account to create.

Open it

Your pet appears at the bottom of the screen and starts wandering. Right-click it for the menu: name it, pick five, open the closet.

Leave it

There is no window to keep open. Tell it to start with Windows and it is there every morning.

Questions

Does the program need to stay open?

The pet is the program. There is no other window. Right-click it and choose Quit when you want it gone.

What does it know about me?

When you usually turn up, your birthday if you told it (day and month only), what it is wearing, and how much you have played with it. All of that sits in one small file on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can my kid have one?

Yes. An adult buys it and a child can be the owner. There is no account for the child, no chat, no messages, and nothing to spend money on inside the pet without the adult.

Mac? Phone?

Windows 10 and 11 today. Mac is next. Phones do not let a pet walk over other apps, so we are not promising one.

Will Windows warn me when I open it?

Until the pet is in the Microsoft Store, Windows may show a warning for a program it has not seen before. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The program touches nothing outside its own folder and one small file for your pet.

What if it is not for me?

Write to us within 14 days and you get your money back.
